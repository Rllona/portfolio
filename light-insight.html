Light Insight

About

Light Insight is a small horror game made for a spanish game jam (Gamegen Game Jam 2º edition).

Roles

Programming, Game Design and Music & Sound Effects

Timeline

1 week (February 2022)

Tools

Unity, Audacity

Game Design

The player takes on the role of a little girl who wakes up in her home, consumed by darkness. To survive, she must escape by following a set of strange rules left behind in mysterious notes.

Characteristics

  • 2D
  • Platforms
  • Hide and Run

Development

The game had to be developed within a short timeframe of just one week. My role focused on lighting and sound, both of which played a key role in shaping the game's atmosphere and mechanics.

Implemented custom shader textures and 2D lighting using Unity's Universal Render Pipeline (URP).

Outcomes

  • Achieved 2nd place out of 24 submissions, as judged by a university panel.
  • Completed the development of a game within a single week. Available on itch.io.
  • Learned to deliver fast prototypes and work effectively under time pressure.
